The housing market keeps growing: tens of thousands of apartments change hands in Tbilisi every year. 2020 was the pandemic low, 2023 a small dip after a record 2022, and the trend is still clearly up. Each one of those buyers and renters plans furniture from 2D photos and floor plans, guessing at scale and fit.

The solution

  1. 01Scan
  2. 02Separate
  3. 03Add &
    Rearrange
Click 1 · Scan: walk around the room taking photos; photogrammetry turns them into a 3D model. Click 2 · Separate: RANSAC finds the largest planes (walls, floor, ceiling) and cuts them away. What is left falls apart into separate islands, and each island is one piece of furniture. Click 3 · Rearrange: every piece can now be moved on its own inside the real room. Click 4 · Add: drag furniture in from any website (Pinterest, a store, a catalogue) and it drops into your room as a 3D object you can move like everything else.
