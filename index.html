The solution

  1. 01Scan
  2. 02Separate
  3. 03Add &
    Rearrange
Click 1 · Scan: walk around the room taking photos; photogrammetry turns them into a 3D model. Click 2 · Separate: RANSAC finds the largest planes (walls, floor, ceiling) and cuts them away. What is left falls apart into separate islands, and each island is one piece of furniture. Click 3 · Rearrange: every piece can now be moved on its own inside the real room. Click 4 · Add: we integrate with Embawood, Saba, Pinterest and more; pick an item and it drops into your room as a 3D object you can move like everything else.
